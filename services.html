Frequently Asked Questions

How much does house cleaning cost in Nairobi?

A standard one-off house clean starts at around KSh 1,500 for a studio and KSh 2,000 for a 1-bedroom, depending on size and extras. Use the estimate calculator on our contact page or message us on WhatsApp for an exact quote.

Do you bring your own cleaning products and equipment?

Yes. Our cleaners arrive with eco-friendly products and professional equipment. If you prefer we use your own products, just let us know when booking.

Are your cleaners background-checked and trained?

Yes. Every Nairobi Cleaning team member is vetted, trained and experienced in residential and commercial cleaning, and we stand behind their work with a satisfaction guarantee.

Which areas do you cover?

We serve Nairobi and its environs — including Westlands, Kilimani, South B, Eastleigh, Kasarani, Ruiru and more. Message us on WhatsApp to confirm availability in your area.

How do I book and pay?

Book through the form on our contact page or directly on WhatsApp. We confirm the date and quote first, and you can pay by M-PESA or cash after the service.
